# Built-in PM practice notes (PM module only)

Product design: clarify the goal and constraints, pick a user segment, map needs and pain points, prioritise one or two problems, generate solutions, choose with explicit trade-offs, define success metrics (north-star plus guardrails).

Root-cause analysis for a metric drop: validate the data, check internal changes (releases, pricing, experiments) and external factors (seasonality, competitors, outages), then segment (platform, version, geography, user cohort) until the cause is isolated. Propose a fix plus monitoring.

Metrics: tie them to the product goal, include adoption, engagement, retention and quality/guardrail metrics, and plan an experiment to measure the effect.

Product strategy: goal first, then users, market, competition and opportunity cost; validate cheaply before building; scope an MVP.
